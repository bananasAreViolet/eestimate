Eestimate is an Estonian party preference polling tracker by bananasAreViolet.
Charts based on weighted (by pollster and date) 30-day average, with LOESS smoothing applied to the displayed trend lines.

Norstat/ÜI polls are given a weight of 0.2 due to their frequency of appearing per week, adding up to a total weight of 0.8 per month.
Emor/ERR polls are given a weight of 1.
Turu-uuringute AS polls are given a weight of 0.7.
